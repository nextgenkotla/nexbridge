Education & Study Abroad Consultancy

From choosing a country to settling in after arrival, we guide students through every stage of studying abroad.

  • Study Abroad CounselingOne-to-one guidance to plan your study abroad journey with confidence.
  • Career CounselingMatch your studies to a career path with real long-term prospects.
  • Academic Profile AssessmentA clear picture of where your grades, tests and experience can take you.
  • Country & University SelectionShortlist the right countries, universities and programs for your profile.
  • University Admission AssistanceStrong, complete applications that give you the best chance of an offer.
  • Application ProcessingWe manage forms, portals, fees and deadlines so nothing is missed.
  • Scholarship GuidanceFind and apply for scholarships and funding you are eligible for.
  • Student Visa ConsultancyExpert guidance through the student visa process for your destination.
  • Visa Documentation GuidanceMake sure every document is correct, complete and consistent.
  • Visa Interview PreparationPractice sessions so you can answer visa interviews clearly and confidently.
  • IELTS & PTE PreparationTargeted test preparation to reach the score your university requires.
  • Pre-Departure OrientationGet ready for travel, arrival and student life before you fly.
  • Post-Arrival GuidanceContinued support while you settle into your new country.

  • Business Advisory & ConsultationExpert advice on the decisions that shape your business.
  • Business PlanningA clear, structured business plan for investors, banks or your own direction.
  • Business Setup & EstablishmentStep-by-step support to set up your business correctly.
  • Company RegistrationRegister your private limited or single member company with confidence.
  • SECP RegistrationIncorporation and filings with the Securities & Exchange Commission of Pakistan.
  • Firm RegistrationRegister your partnership firm with the Registrar of Firms.

  • Business Development & Strategic PlanningPlan growth, new markets and partnerships with a clear strategy.
