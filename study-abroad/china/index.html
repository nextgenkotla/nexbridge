Study in China

Fast-rising universities, strong scholarships and English-taught MBBS programs.

Introduction to China

China's leading universities have risen quickly in global rankings and attract large numbers of international students. Strong government scholarships and English-taught programs, including medicine, make China a popular choice for students from Pakistan.

Education system

Bachelor's degrees usually take four years, and five to six years for medicine. Master's degrees take two to three years and doctorates three to four. Programs are offered in Chinese and, increasingly, in English.

Available programs

  • Medicine (MBBS)
  • Engineering and technology
  • Computer science
  • Business and economics
  • Chinese language
  • Agriculture and natural sciences

Admission requirements

  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Passport
  • English or Chinese (HSK) proficiency depending on the program
  • Study plan or research proposal for postgraduate study
  • Physical examination form

Tuition fee overview

Tuition is moderate and varies by university and program. Living costs are lower than in most Western destinations, though major cities cost more.

Fees change every year. We share current figures for the universities on your shortlist.

Scholarships & funding

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

Scholarships covering tuition, accommodation, a stipend and medical insurance.

University and provincial scholarships

Many universities and provinces offer their own scholarships for international students.

Application process

  1. 1

    Shortlist universities and scholarships

  2. 2

    Prepare documents and the physical examination form

  3. 3

    Apply to universities and scholarship programs

  4. 4

    Receive your admission letter and JW201/JW202 form

  5. 5

    Apply for your student visa

  6. 6

    Travel and complete residence registration

Student visa information

Long-term students apply for an X1 student visa using their admission letter and the JW201 or JW202 form, then convert it to a residence permit after arrival. Short-term students use an X2 visa.

Visa decisions are made only by the relevant embassy or immigration authority. Requirements change, and we check the current rules for every application.

Frequently asked questions

Are Chinese MBBS degrees recognized in Pakistan?

Graduates must meet the requirements of the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council. Choose a university and program that is recognized. We check this for you.

Do I need to learn Chinese?

Not for English-taught programs, though basic Chinese is very useful in daily life and clinical training.
